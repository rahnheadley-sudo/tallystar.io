# Tallystar

Astrology compatibility, with the receipts.

Every score in Tallystar shows its working: the exact angles between two charts, how close each one is, and what it adds or takes away. Nothing is a verdict. You can check every line.

**$9.99 once. No subscription required. No ads. No account. Your birth data stays on your phone. Anything included in your Unlock stays yours.**

## What you get

- Birth charts for you and the people you care about, worked out on your phone.
- Compatibility scores by theme (communication, chemistry, and more), each with its contributing aspects listed.
- Everything you need to see your compatibility is free: every score for every pair, with its working, rare finds and Paper share cards. The Unlock adds deeper readings, the full question library and the Night card style.
- Honest handling of unknown birth times: when we cannot know, we show a range and say so.
