# Tallystar privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Tallystar is an astrology compatibility app. It is built so that we never receive your data. This page says what that means in practice.

## The short version

- We collect nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads.
- The birth details you enter stay on your phone. We never see them.
- Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup or share a card.
- You can delete one person, or everything, at any time. It is immediate and complete.

## What the app stores, and where

| What | Where it lives | Does it leave your phone? |
|---|---|---|
| People you add: name or nickname, label (for example partner or friend), birth date, birth time and how certain it is, birth place | The app's private storage on your phone | Only if you export a backup |
| Charts and comparison scores worked out from those details | The same private storage | No |
| Which purchases you own | On your phone, checked through Apple or Google | The purchase itself is handled by Apple or Google, not by us |
| Place searches | Held in memory only, matched against a list bundled in the app | No |
| Your theme choice and other settings | On your phone | No |

All astrology calculations and all text you read are produced on your phone. The app makes no calls to any service of ours, and it uses no outside service to calculate anything.

## Things you can choose to send somewhere

- **Export a backup.** You can save one file with everything you entered. The app shows how many people it contains and reminds you to keep it private. It is a plain file, so anyone who has it can read it. Where it goes is up to you. We do not upload it anywhere.
- **Share a card.** The app can draw an image to share. It never includes anyone's birth date, time or place, and it hides names unless you turn them on. Where you post it is up to you.

## Other people's details

You may enter details for people other than yourself. Please only add people who would be fine with it. Because the details stay on your phone, they are not passed to anyone by us.

## Purchases

The optional one-time Unlock is sold by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle payment and receipts under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details or your identity.

## Backups made by your phone

Your phone's own backup, such as iCloud or Google's backup, may include the app's storage if you have those turned on. That is a copy you control under your own account. We cannot read it. Deleting data in the app does not remove copies in your phone's old backups; those age out on the schedule your phone sets.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete a person:** removes them and every comparison involving them.
- **Delete all data:** removes every person, comparison and setting from the app. Your purchase stays and can be restored through the store's "Restore purchases".
- Uninstalling the app removes its storage from your phone.

Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to ask us to delete and nothing for us to send you. Your export file is your copy.

## Children

Tallystar is for adults aged 18 and over. It deals with adult relationships and is not meant for children.

## Where the law applies

We do not receive or process your personal data on any system of ours, so rights such as access, correction and deletion are met by the app's own export and delete tools. We have written this policy to the standards of privacy law in Australia, the European Union and United Kingdom, and California, but this page is not legal advice.

## Tracking and this website

The app does not track you, so there is nothing for a browser's "Do Not Track" setting to switch off. These web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits under its own privacy policy. We add no analytics or cookies to these pages.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. The app is designed so that no change can start collecting data silently: any collection would need new code and a new store release.
