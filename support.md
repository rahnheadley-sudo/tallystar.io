## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. There are no accounts.

**Where is my data?** On your phone only. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

**How do I move to a new phone?** Settings, then Export everything. Save the file, move it to the new phone, then use Import there. Import adds people it has not seen before and never overwrites what is already there.

**How do I delete everything?** Settings, then Delete all data. It asks you to confirm and cannot be undone.

**I bought the Unlock on another phone.** Use Restore purchases in the Unlock screen, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

**Why does my chart have a range instead of a single number?** If you do not know a birth time, the app cannot place the Moon or the rising sign exactly, so it shows what it can and says what it cannot.

**How is the score worked out?** Every score shows its working: the exact angles between two charts and what each adds. See How it works inside the app.

**A refund.** Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Ask them using their refund process.

## Reporting a problem

Tell us your phone model, system version, the app version (Settings, then Legal and attributions) and what you did before it went wrong.
